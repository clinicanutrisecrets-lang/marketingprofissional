# Anúncio — imersão gratuita em nutrigenética

Público: **nutricionista que trabalha com saúde integrativa**. Promessa:
enxergar o paciente inteiro no emagrecimento, além da bioimpedância.

Material: vídeo do consultório + vídeo multicena (laboratório, chá,
bioimpedância, cozinha) + uma cena nova de convite. ~32s.

---

## O gancho

A dor do profissional não é "não sei emagrecer paciente" — é **o paciente
emagrece errado**. Daí o gancho:

> A paciente emagreceu.
> E perdeu **massa magra**, cabelo e sono junto.
>
> A bioimpedância mostrou.
> Mas não explicou **por quê**.

Isso abre a lacuna que o resto do anúncio preenche — e usa a cena da
balança como prova visual, não como enfeite.

nem "prevê". Genética é predisposição, não destino (a própria Aline
escreve isso no laudo dela).

## Cena nova — o convite

Plano de olhar direto na câmera, sem falar: é o que dá autoridade num CTA
e deixa o texto respirar por cima.

```
SCENE CONTEXT
A nutritionist at her consulting room desk finishes reviewing a case, closes the folder in front of her and looks up directly into the camera, holding the gaze.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@image1: the woman, around 40, professional. Her face, hair, build and skin 100% match the reference.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: the edge of a wooden desk with a closed folder and a ceramic cup. Midground: the woman seated behind the desk. Background: a soft-focus consulting room with a plant and a shelf. Camera directly in front of her at seated eye level. Daylight from a large window on the left.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
She is already seated, eyes down on the closed folder, both hands resting flat on the desk on either side of it.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, the camera does not cut on its own.

OPTICS
Medium close-up, 65mm equivalent, shallow depth of field.

CAMERA
Handheld with very slight natural breathing, very slow push in.

ACTION
She rests her right hand on the closed folder for a moment. Then she lifts her eyes from the desk straight into the lens and holds the gaze until the end of the shot. Her hands stay where they are.

PERFORMANCE
Calm confidence held in the eyes. The corners of her mouth lift only slightly at the very end, lips closed the whole time. One slow blink. A micro-pause before she looks up. Pore-level skin realism with visible pores, fine lines and slight asymmetry, no smoothing, no retouching. Living eyes with soft catch-lights from the window. Natural loose strands of hair.

PHYSICS
Contact shadows under the folder and the cup. The folder sits with real weight on the desk.

LIGHTING
Soft daylight from a large window on the left, gentle fill on the right side of her face, background one stop darker.

WARDROBE
A plain professional blouse with natural creases, worn not new.

STYLE
Photoreal, observational documentary style, neutral color grading, true to life colors, natural film grain.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Her face matches the reference. Lips stay closed, she never speaks. Both hands stay fully in frame, never half-cut by the edge, and she does not open the folder, write or gesture. The folder, cup and any paper stay soft with no readable text, numbers or logos. No other people in frame. The eye contact with the lens holds through the second half of the shot.
```

**Onde entra o texto**: o olhar direto ocupa a segunda metade do plano —
é ali que o selo da imersão aparece, com o rosto dela sustentando a
chamada.
