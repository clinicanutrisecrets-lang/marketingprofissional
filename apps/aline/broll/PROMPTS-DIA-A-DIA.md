# Prompts de dia a dia

A vida fora do consultório. Isto é o que falta pro perfil não parecer só
clínica: alguém que acorda, toma café, anda descalça e vê o sol descer.
Serve de abertura de reel, de respiro no meio de um assunto pesado e de
fundo pra frase de longevidade.

Cada bloco é inteiro. Copia do começo ao fim, cola, gera.

Todos são **mudos**, de lábio fechado, porque a narração entra depois.
Cada um vem com uma expressão escolhida, e as quatro linhas pra trocar
estão em `PROMPTS-ALINE-EM-CENA.md`.

Estes **gastam crédito**, porque você aparece. No fim do arquivo tem três
variações sem rosto, dessas mesmas cenas, que saem no ilimitado do site.

Antes de gerar: duração mais curta disponível, 9:16, resolução máxima,
**"enhance prompt" desligado**, sem áudio.

## 8. Os pés descalços na grama

```
SCENE CONTEXT
Four short shots of bare feet on grass in a garden at golden hour, no face, no person above the knees.

LOCATION MAP
A lawn of fresh green grass in a quiet garden. Camera low, close to the ground. Warm low sun from the side, long soft shadows on the grass.

FORMAT MODE
Four separate shots with hard cuts between them, no dissolves. Same lawn, same light and same lens in all four.

OPTICS
Close-up, 50mm equivalent, shallow depth of field.

CAMERA
Static locked off, no movement.

ACTION
Scene 1 (0-5s): bare feet standing still on the grass, the blades bending slightly under them.
Hard cut.
Scene 2 (5-10s): one bare foot takes a slow step forward and presses into the grass.
Hard cut.
Scene 3 (10-15s): extreme macro of blades of grass with the low sun behind them, glowing at the edges.
Hard cut.
Scene 4 (15-20s): the two bare feet standing still again, warm light across the lawn.

PHYSICS
The grass bends under the weight and springs back, it is never flattened into a patch. The feet stay in contact with the ground at all times.

LIGHTING
Warm low golden hour sun from the side.

STYLE
Photoreal cinematography, visible natural texture, true to life colors, fine grain.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The feet are anatomically correct, five toes each, bare and clean, nails short and unpainted. Only the feet and the lower legs are visible, nothing above the knees, no face, no other people. No text or labels.
```

POSITIVE LOCKS
